🚗 Getting around

  • Palermo (Jul 23–28): no car. Taxi or pre-booked transfer from PMO (Uber coverage is limited); hotel is inside the ZTL
  • Car Jul 28 → Aug 5. Never leave luggage in the car at tourist sites
  • Ortigia is ZTL: ask the hotel to register the plate for luggage drop and which lot to use (Molo Sant'Antonio or Talete). Fallback: park at Molo Sant'Antonio on the mainland, ~5 min walk in
  • Taormina is very walkable; car needed for Etna and day trips
  • Autostrada tolls: keep cash or card ready · Many gas stations close 1–4 PM

🥗 Vegetarian guide (Elisabeth)

  • ✅ Pasta alla Norma, caponata, parmigiana di melanzane, panelle, pistachio pesto pasta, granita, cassata
  • Antipasto spreads are very veggie-friendly — always ask "senza carne"
  • MOON (Ortigia) is the standout veggie dinner of the trip

🌡 Weather

  • Late July–early Aug: 30–36°C (86–97°F), hotter inland; Taormina is cooler thanks to elevation
  • Morning sightseeing is essential for exposed sites (Agrigento, Etna)
  • Best beach time: late afternoon or early morning
